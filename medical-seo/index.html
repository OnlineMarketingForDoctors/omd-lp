The nine step high performance process

What we actually do, in the order we actually do it.

No secret sauce, no black hat, nothing that puts your domain at risk. This is the same sequence we have run for clinics since 2011, refined every time it met a new market.

01

Consultation

We work out which treatment makes you the most money, which one you want to be known for, and who your ideal patient actually is. Everything downstream depends on getting this right.

02

Technical audit

A full crawl of the site. Speed, indexation, structure, duplicate content, the thin pages quietly dragging everything down. We find what is broken before we build anything on top of it.

03

Conversion review

Traffic to a site that does not convert is a bonfire. We review the journey from landing to enquiry and fix the leaks first, because doubling a 1% conversion rate is cheaper than doubling the traffic.

04

Keyword research

We map the terms real patients use, by treatment and by location, and separate the ones with commercial intent from the ones that only look impressive in a report.

05

Competitor analysis

What the clinic down the road ranks for and you do not. Which of their pages earn the traffic, where their links come from, and the gap you can take while they are not looking.

06

On page optimisation

Titles, structure, schema, internal linking, image handling, Core Web Vitals. The unglamorous work that decides whether Google trusts the site enough to rank it.

07

Off page and links

Placements on genuine health, lifestyle and news publications. One authoritative link beats a thousand cheap ones, and we have never used a private blog network. Not once.

08

Content and local SEO

Long form treatment pages written to E-E-A-T standards, plus Google Business Profiles restructured per location so your clinics rank in Maps instead of competing with each other.

09

Reporting

Monthly, in plain English, measured against the benchmarks we set on day one. Enquiries and bookings first. You will always know what your money bought.

Most agencies learn medical advertising rules the hard way, using your clinic as the classroom. We have worked under AHPRA, the GMC, the ASA and HIPAA since 2011, and the constraints are built into how we write rather than checked at the end.

It matters more than it sounds. A page that breaches guidance does not just get pulled. It takes its rankings with it, and rebuilding that authority takes months you cannot afford.

FAQ

The questions everyone asks on the call.

How long before I see anything?

It depends where you are starting. If the site has some history and authority, movement usually shows in the first three months and meaningful enquiry growth from month four onwards. If we are building from nothing, the infrastructure alone takes the first couple of months before rankings start to respond.

Anybody quoting you a faster timeline is either guessing or selling something they cannot deliver.

How much does medical SEO cost?

It depends on the competitiveness of your treatments, how many locations you run, the current state of the site and how fast you want to move. A single site chiropractor in one town is a very different job from a dental group with four practices, or a clinic going after facelifts in central London.

Be wary of any agency publishing a fixed price list for SEO. It means they are running the same template over every business regardless of what it needs. We will give you a real number on the call once we have looked at your market.

Can you guarantee I will rank number one?

No, and neither can anybody else. Nobody outside Google controls the algorithm, and any agency putting a specific position in writing is telling you something they cannot know.

What we can show you is a decade of case studies in your sector with the clinics named and the numbers attached, which is a considerably better basis for a decision than a promise.

Do you use private blog networks or anything that could get me penalised?

Never. Not once, in fourteen years. PBNs are the cheap shortcut most of this industry quietly relies on, and when Google finds them the site does not slip a few places, it disappears.

We build links through genuine placements on real publications. It is slower, it costs more, and it does not evaporate the moment an algorithm updates.

Will my website need to change?

Almost certainly, yes. In most of the case studies on this page the site was rebuilt or substantially rewritten, because the conversion rate was the real constraint rather than the traffic.

Sending more visitors to a page that does not convert is the most expensive mistake in this whole discipline. We fix that first.

What about ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

It is already changing where enquiries come from. Patients ask an assistant which clinic to use and act on the answer without ever seeing a traditional results page.

The work that earns those citations is closely related to good organic SEO, genuine authority, thorough content and clean structured data, but it is not identical. It is included in what we do, and the AI search section above shows current examples for our clients.

We rely on GP and consultant referrals. Is this relevant to us?

More than you would expect, and it applies as much to a rheumatologist or neurologist taking GP referrals as to a cosmetic clinic. Referred patients still look you up before they book, and what they find decides whether the referral converts or quietly evaporates.

A strong search presence also wins you the patients who never got a referral in the first place, which is a channel most referral led practices leave entirely to their competitors.

Who actually does the work?

Our own team, never outsourced. You get a named account manager who knows your market, and the same specialists working on your campaign month after month.
