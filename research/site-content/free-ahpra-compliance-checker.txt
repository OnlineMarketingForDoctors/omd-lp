### Why It Matters:
AHPRA (Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency) enforces strict rules on healthcare advertising. If your website doesn’t comply, you risk penalties, complaints, and loss of patient trust.
### Common breaches include:
- Misleading claims
- Use of before & after photos without warnings
- Unqualified testimonials
- Lack of risk disclosures
