### FAQ
### You’ve got questions, ﻿We’ve got answers
When can I expect to see results?
It depends on your starting point. If you’ve already done some digital marketing, you’ll likely see faster results. However, if you’re starting from scratch, it may take a little longer as we first need to set up the complete patient generation infrastructure. Once your campaign is fully launched, you’ll typically start seeing tangible results from the second month onwards.
How do I know the campaign will work and bring the results we’re looking for?
From the start, we’ll establish benchmarks based on your current performance and measure progress every month against both these benchmarks and your targeted KPIs.
You’ll see consistent improvements in your Google local map rankings , Google AI Overviews , organic search visibility , and other AI search platforms . These will translate into more enquiries , higher-quality leads , and increased bookings for your main procedures, treatments or products.
What budget do I need to start with?
Marketing shouldn’t be seen as a cost — it’s an investment that delivers measurable returns. If the campaign performs well, increasing your budget simply means increasing your profits.
We’ll measure your ROI monthly , giving you full transparency on how well the campaign is performing so you can confidently reinvest, knowing your money is working harder than in any other marketing channel.
Typically, you should start with a minimum budget of $AUD 3,000 (approx. £GBP 1,500 / €EUR 1,700 / $USD 2,000) per digital channel (e.g., SEO, Paid Per Click Ads, or Social Ads). The final budget depends on the number of procedures/services/treatments you want to promote and the number of channels involved.
How can I make sure the campaign is successful?
For your campaigns to be successful, it’s essential to maintain a high conversion rate from enquiry to consultation, and from consultation to procedure booking (ideally at least 50%).
Here are other key success factors:
- Build a strong sales process with a well-trained team dedicated to handling patient enquiries and consultations.
- Showcase impressive before-and-after results , patient case studies , and authentic reviews to boost trust and credibility.
- Keep your online reputation in great shape by checking Reddit and other review sites, and responding thoughtfully to any negative comments.
- Clearly communicate your unique points of difference and value propositions .
- Offer accessible payment plan options for patients who need financial flexibility.
By combining these internal strengths with our proven marketing systems, your campaign will perform at its highest potential.
How long is the campaign engagement?
We recommend a minimum 12-month commitment for the first year. This period is crucial for building the marketing infrastructure including website revamp, social media optimisation, conversion rate improvement, and content development (such as patient reviews and case studies).
Most clients experience significant, exponential growth from the second year onwards , once the foundation built in the first year starts compounding results.
What if it doesn’t work out? What happens?
Before we launch, we outline clear performance projections and set realistic expectations for your campaign. If you’ve previously run digital marketing campaigns, you’ll likely notice improvements even faster.
Over the past decade, every client who has followed our recommendations precisely has seen measurable results. However, if you’ve implemented all our strategies exactly as advised and still don’t experience any improvement, you may contact us to review your case and request an early termination of the agreement.
