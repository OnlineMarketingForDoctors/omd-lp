### CHALLENGE
Sinclair Dermatology (SD) is a medium-sized company that is led by internationally renowned dermatologist, Professor Rodney Sinclair who has been operating for over 20 years. The client was looking to build a new website that accurately reflects their vision, mission, who they are and the services they are providing.
### SOLUTION
At OMD we designed and developed a complex website that helped Sinclair Dermatology achieve the following:
- Generate more GP referrals
- Generate clinical trials patients
- Recruit sponsors for clinical trials
- Increase patients to visit their salon
- Increase patients to visit their dermal clinic
- Be a centre for treatment for all dermatological needs – the go-to place when you think of dermatology.
- Be a workplace other dermatologists want to be part of
- Convert page visits into bookings/leads
