### CHALLENGE
Marvel Clinic offers hair transplant and restoration services in Mumbai, India and has one of the most attractive prices in the area while offering outstanding results for its patients. They came to the OMD team with one goal in mind: a wealth of new patients. Dr Malay Mehta wanted to grow his patient roster faster and more efficiently.
After consulting with the team at Marvel, we did a thorough evaluation of their existing website and SEO performance.
The OMD team identified opportunities for growth, and drafted an ROI-focused plan to partner with Marvel Clinic and achieve the growth they were looking for.
### SOLUTION
One of the main Paid Per Click advertising strategies is to build a dedicated landing page without distractions and a single-focusedDrMumbaito action. The landing page helps answer all possible questions that prospects might have. The landing page helps increase conversions by 426.52%.
After an in-depth discussion with the Marvel team, we designed, built, and launched a sleek, custom landing page, tailored to the PPC campaign, to ensure they would start to get optimum results. We worked closely with Dr. Mehta to make sure the messaging and targeting were just right. Then we launched a comprehensive PPC campaign to target Mumbay, India.
The Google Ads campaigns generated a total of 477 conversions in just 1 month at a cost as low as $2 per conversion.
