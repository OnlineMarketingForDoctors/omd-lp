### Parking Information
Our office is located at B407/440 Harris Street. To access the visitor parking area, please head to the back of the building, situated at 291 Pyrmont Street. There, you'll find the gate and please press 2407 and then hit the "Bell" button to ring the office. We will open the parking gate for you, and then follow the signs to the visitor parking area up the ramp in front of you.
Once you've parked your vehicle, take the lift to level 4, which is the ground floor exit to Harris Street. Exit the building and then turn right, and you'll find our ground floor office as the second door on your right-hand side.
We look forward to welcoming you to our office! If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out to us.
