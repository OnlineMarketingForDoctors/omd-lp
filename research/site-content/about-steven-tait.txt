Hi, I’m Steven Tait.
I'm a passionate educator and advocate in the power of effective marketing.
Far from relying on deceptive practices and over-promising and under-delivering, I use a clear statistic driven strategy to inform and drive digital marketing initiatives that I have used successfully to build and expand companies across a range of industries; from vehicle hires to international importers to plastic surgeons.
My most recent speaking gig was at the Non-Surgical Symposium in the Gold Coast, discussing the latest medical regulations in Australia, and how you can successfully navigate these regulations while still driving more leads your way.  If you'd like to learn more about this topic, check out our free podcasts and other resources to get the latest strategies you can use to drive more success your way.
