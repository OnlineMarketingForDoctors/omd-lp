### Challenge
When Australian Industrial Vacuum was on-boarded to King Kong, they were previously running PPC ads as well as Facebook-boosted posts in-house which were providing them with a large number of calls but the tracking on the back end needed to be corrected so they could never get accurate results and scale. Once onboarded to King Kong the team sprinkled their magic and set up the correct conversion tracking and systems in place to take this campaign to the moon! Google ads conversion rate was sitting at 1.80% before and currently for 2024 the conversion rate is now at 5.19! That’s an increase of 188%!
Paid Search Conversion Rate
### King Kong’s Approach
We dialled into the specific top-of-funnel and bottom-of-funnel keywords for industrial vacuum cleaners which over time have skyrocketed their organic search traffic and sales by targeting these highly relevant keywords.
The King Kong Approach employs a hybrid strategy starting with On-page work and then progressing to Off-page work. For Google Ads, the specialists look at which key terms are getting the highest conversions and customers and optimise the campaign and strategy from there whilst also looking at the previous ad account to see what has worked and has not worked in the past.
