### Background
My Muscle Chef is one of the worldwide biggest ready made meal providers. Their high-protein, nutrition-packed offerings were making waves with health-conscious Aussies. But in 2017, they faced a major roadblock: almost no visibility for generic keywords. In a hyper-competitive industry, brand alone wasn’t enough.
They needed to dominate organic search, or get buried by competitors.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Despite strong brand traction, My Muscle Chef was nowhere to be found on Google for high-volume, intent-rich keywords. The market was flooded with meal prep services all fighting for the same digital shelf space.
To become the go-to choice for healthy convenience, they had to rise through the rankings, fast.
### Our Approach
We overhauled their SEO foundation from the ground up.
First, we helped to optimise their website architecture. Cleaner hierarchy. Smarter internal linking. Lightning-fast navigation. Everything is engineered to make Google’s bots and real users fall in love.
Next, we crafted a targeted keyword strategy focused on bottom-of-funnel buyers: people already hungry and ready to convert. At the same time, we layered in high-intent informational content to capture early interest and build authority.
From product pages to educational blogs, we aligned every word with what health-focused shoppers were searching for, and made sure My Muscle Chef showed up first.
### Results
With their SEO engine firing, My Muscle Chef surged up the rankings and their organic traffic climbed 54%, whilst their keyword visibility exploded by 117%.
This wasn’t just more traffic, it was the right traffic. Qualified, purchase-ready customers who were actively searching for a better way to eat well without the prep time.
Today, My Muscle Chef isn’t just competing. They’re leading the charge.
