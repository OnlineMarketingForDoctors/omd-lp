### Background
Let’s Get Care is Australia’s first self-managed Home Care Package provider, delivering in-home care services tailored to the individual needs of older Australians. Their model empowers clients to choose who delivers their care, giving them more flexibility, control, and better value from their Home Care Package.
They operate in a highly competitive space dominated by traditional providers. While their offering is unique, it’s not always easy to understand at first glance. Their success depends not just on visibility, but on educating the market about how self-managed care works, and why it’s a better alternative.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Let’s Get Care faced a common but critical problem: the aged care market is saturated with large, established players using generic messaging. Their innovative model was often overlooked or misunderstood by potential clients. To succeed, they needed more than just clicks, they needed clarity, cut-through, and a consistent pipeline of qualified leads.
They came to King Kong looking for a strategic advertising partner who could lead the charge, shape market perception, and scale their lead generation without sending costs soaring.
### Our Approach
We developed a comprehensive advertising strategy across both Google and Facebook, designed to increase lead volume while clearly communicating Let’s Get Care’s point of difference.
On Google, we uncovered untapped keyword opportunities beyond the traditional aged care terms and combined these with aggressive bidding strategies for high-intent search phrases. This helped us expand into new areas of demand while also outperforming competitors on the most valuable terms.
On Facebook, we built campaigns that distilled the core message of self-managed care into sharp, benefit-led ads, educating the market while driving action.
### Results
We saw a 905% increase in monthly leads.
Cost efficiency also improved dramatically. With a 44.7% decrease in cost-per-lead on Google, the business could scale without compromising profitability.
