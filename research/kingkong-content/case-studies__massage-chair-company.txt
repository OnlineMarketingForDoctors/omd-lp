### Background
This Massage Chair company is a family-owned business, based in Melbourne, that specialises in high-end massage chairs. With showrooms and warehouses located across the country, they offer a diverse range of chairs designed to provide a realistic and therapeutic experience. Their premium selection includes innovative 4D massage chairs and models featuring Zero Gravity recline, deep tissue massage capabilities, and specialised rollers for foot reflexology.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
This Massage Chair company faced a significant hurdle: a lack of robust goal tracking and the right strategy to grow their business through digital. This meant their path to business scalability was less of a strategic roadmap and more of a shot in the dark. Their ambitious goal to double their revenue remained just that, a goal, without the data-driven foundation needed to achieve it with precision.
### Our Approach
To hit this Massage Chair company’ revenue targets, we deployed an end‑to‑end performance strategy designed to dominate both traffic and conversion. We tore down their entire sales funnel, rebuilt it for maximum efficiency, and ran continuous A/B split testing to eliminate friction at every stage. This was paired with hard‑hitting direct response copy, amplified through hyper‑targeted Google and Meta Ads campaigns engineered to reach high‑intent buyers with precision. Every decision was driven by ongoing Conversion Rate Optimisation, ensuring increased spend translated into explosive ROAS, not wasted traffic.
