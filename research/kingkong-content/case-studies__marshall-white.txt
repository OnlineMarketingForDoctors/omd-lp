### Background
Marshall White is one of Melbourne’s most recognised names in prestige real estate, with a legacy of premium property sales across high-demand areas like Brighton, Toorak, and Armadale.
But while their offline brand was strong, their digital footprint told a different story, particularly in key suburbs. They weren’t ranking. They weren’t getting found. And they were leaving money on the table.
Their goal was to increase their Google Rankings for key suburbs.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Despite their authority in the market, Marshall White had no suburb-specific SEO structure in place.
Their website was missing dedicated pages for key suburbs, and they weren’t showing up when locals searched for agents or listings in their core service areas.
### King Kong’s Approach
We implemented our proven strategies to give this campaign a full overhaul.
First, we thoroughly analysed their website’s architecture, pinpointing relevant keywords and developing a robust on-page SEO strategy, from optimising meta title tags, building internal linking strategies, to creating 43+ additional suburb-focused SEO pages.
We then complemented this with our aggressive off-page strategies to add fuel to the fire.
All content was written using our proven direct-response copy frameworks, targeting keywords that convert strangers into new leads.
