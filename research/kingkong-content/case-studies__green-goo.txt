### Background
Green Goo was founded to offer consumers truly effective, natural mould removal solutions, unlike typical supermarket products that only treat surfaces. They started with a focus on Tea Tree and Eucalyptus oil, developing their “Green-Goo mould buster” that actively kills mould at its root. Their spray works by making mould consume it, ensuring complete eradication. Driven by customer feedback, Green Goo has since expanded its product line to tackle various household needs using other natural essential oils.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
This client sought a new agency after experiencing limitations in scaling their Meta and Google Ads campaigns with three previous providers. Despite utilising both major advertising platforms, they struggled to achieve significant growth and are now aiming to triple their business, indicating a need for a more effective and scalable advertising strategy.
### Solution
Green Goo partnered with King Kong to create a robust advertising campaign that included Direct-Response Copywriting, Conversion Rate Optimisation, Google Ads, and Meta Ads.
We began with a deep dive into their funnel and conversion processes while analysing their buyer’s journey keeping in mind potential conversion drop-off points and areas to make changes to their check out processes.
### Our Approach
We combined King Kong’s advertising magic with a “lightning in a bottle” heavily conversion-focused landing page to leverage both advertising mediums, Meta & Google, to the fullest.
Our strategy focused on the natural, plant based formulas from Green Goo and the benefits of a more health-conscious mould killing solution. Plenty of before & after images and social proofs support the campaign’s claims and address concerns of using the traditional bleach cleaners or Green Goo.
Our signature King Kong long-form direct response copy crafted ads that literally drug users into the landing page in such large numbers that our clients had to reevaluate their supply chain.

### Results
We helped mould killing business Green Goo revolutionise their pipeline and blow through the roadblocks they had hit with previous marketing companies. Strategically growing their conversions and revenues by 182% on Google and 92% on Meta. A brand new conversion-focused landing page and some ‘secret sauce’ copy helped drive a 107% increase in their conversion rate. This pushed them from $330K to over $750K since coming onboard with King Kong setting them well along the path to $1M.
