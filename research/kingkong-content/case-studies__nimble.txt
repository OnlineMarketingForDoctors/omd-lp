### Background
Nimble, a prominent player in Australia’s short-term loan sector, sought to elevate its online presence and lead generation capabilities through organic search.
Partnering with King Kong, they embarked on a journey to revitalise their digital footprint.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
When King Kong first engaged with Nimble, the company faced a critical challenge: negligible visibility for pivotal keywords like “Payday Loans,” “Cash Loans,” and “Emergency Loans.”
Despite significant investments in traditional advertising channels such as television, their organic traffic was primarily confined to branded keywords. With a strategic shift away from above-the-line marketing, Nimble’s organic traffic plummeted, necessitating a radical overhaul.
### King Kong’s Approach
King Kong’s SEO experts dove into action, meticulously laying the groundwork for Nimble’s resurgence. They undertook a comprehensive audit, optimising website architecture, identifying relevant keywords, and fortifying on-page SEO elements.
Complemented by a robust off-page strategy, this approach aimed to enhance Nimble’s domain authority and relevance across search engine results pages (SERPs).
