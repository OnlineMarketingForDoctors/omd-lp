### Background
Koikas Acoustics is a Sydney residential and commercial Sound Control Acoustic engineering company.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
They came to us after being in business for 25 years because although word-of-mouth referrals were coming in… they were only getting 1-2 leads every couple of months on their website and wanted a strategy to help increase this.
### King Kong’s Approach
We helped Koikas Acoustics transform its approach to lead generation by leveraging a robust SEO strategy.
Traditionally they relied on referrals but we applied our battle-tested strategies to skyrocket organic search traffic, Google rankings, organic search leads and revenue.
We started by creating a new strategy for Koikas Acoustics by optimising their website architecture, mapping out the most relevant keywords and establishing a strong on-page SEO presence and added fuel to it with an extensive off-page strategy to increase organic search traffic and organic leads, domain authority and relevancy across the SERP’s.
Our Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) strategy focused on their dream customer targeting relevant and high-intent keywords that resulted in high-quality leads.  We combined our battle-tested direct-response copy, SEO content, our team of specialised experts, 11 special herbs and spices and worked with the extraordinary Koikas Acoustics team which resulted in skyrocketed sales and has generated millions in revenue during our ongoing 5+ year partnership.
