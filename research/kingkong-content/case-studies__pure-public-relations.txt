### Background
Pure Public Relations is a PR agency specialising in helping clients enhance their reputation, secure media placements, and achieve growth.
Their expertise spans businesses of all sizes, including charities, industry bodies, and non-profits.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
When Pure Public Relations came to us, they had minimal Google Page 1 visibility, with only 10 keywords ranking. They wanted to drastically increase their rankings, drive more website leads, and establish themselves as the go-to PR agency in their industry.
Their goal was clear: to generate more enquiries by boosting brand awareness and appearing as a top choice for PR services. As they put it:
“We came on board with King Kong because we wanted to grow the number of enquiries we were receiving by increasing awareness of our agency and having us be high up on the list of options for those considering PR. We are great at building trust and fostering a strong reputation and building authority in our business as a leader – and King Kong brings eyeballs to that.”
### Our Approach
We implemented our time-tested strategy to grow Pure Public Relations’ organic search traffic and leads, transforming their minimal presence on Google into a dominant one.
First, we overhauled their website’s architecture to improve search engine rankings and user experience. We identified high-intent keywords and established a strong on-page SEO framework.
Next, we fuelled growth with an aggressive off-page SEO strategy. By building high-quality backlinks and increasing domain authority, we positioned Pure Public Relations as a leader in search engine results, driving organic leads and traffic across the board.
### Results
We collaborated closely with the Pure Public Relations team to implement our proven SEO strategy, driving significant growth in Google rankings, traffic, leads, and sales. Throughout the partnership, we worked to optimise their sales processes, ensuring a seamless conversion of online leads into revenue.
