### Background
Aussie Environmental is a leading provider of eco-friendly products and services in Australia. They cater to both individuals and businesses, offering a wide range of sustainable solutions aimed at preserving the environment.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Aussie Environmental faced the challenge of standing out in a competitive market dominated by nationwide competitors. They needed a robust online presence to increase organic traffic, boost their eCommerce conversion rate, and significantly drive up their organic revenue within a year.
### King Kong’s Approach
We partnered with Aussie Environmental to craft a powerhouse strategy focused on SEO and website optimisation. Our mission was clear: dominate the search rankings, skyrocket organic traffic, and turn their website into a conversion machine.
First, we dove deep into an extensive keyword analysis, pinpointing the high-potential search terms that would bring in qualified traffic. We revamped their existing content, adding new, high-quality material tailored to these keywords. Technical SEO improvements ensured the site was a favorite with search engines, while authoritative backlinks boosted their domain authority.
Next, we transformed their website. The design overhaul provided a seamless user experience, with lightning-fast speed and mobile responsiveness. We streamlined navigation, making it effortless for customers to find and purchase products. Continuous A/B testing allowed us to fine-tune and perfect the eCommerce conversion rate.
### Results
Our strategic approach delivered phenomenal results. Aussie Environmental saw a 50% surge in organic traffic and a 51% jump in page 1 rankings. The optimised website didn’t just attract visitors—it converted them, with a 35% increase in the eCommerce conversion rate. This powerful combination generated a staggering $1,233,000 in organic revenue, a 155% year-on-year increase. We didn’t just help them compete; we helped them dominate, outranking nationwide competitors and securing top positions in search results.
