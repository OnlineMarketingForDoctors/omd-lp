### Background
Scotland Titles, a company specializing in personalized Scottish titles, entered a competitive niche market in 2020 with no brand awareness or customer base, posing significant challenges to their growth.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Establish product and brand awareness, gain market share, and achieve profitability early on in a niche market dominated by established competitors who have been active for years.
### Solution
To address the challenges faced by Scotland Titles, King Kong implemented a comprehensive performance marketing solution. They developed an Ecommerce Microsite to maximize conversion rate and employed SEO, Paid Search, Facebook Ads, and YouTube Ads to drive targeted traffic and revenue. Direct response copywriting and constant Conversion Rate Optimization techniques were utilized to optimize ad campaigns and the website’s user experience. Long-form direct response ad copies on Meta were used to generate awareness and interest in the unique gift offerings. Through these strategies, King Kong positioned Scotland Titles as a leader in the niche market, resulting in significant revenue growth and market dominance.
### King Kong’s Approach
King Kong analyzed the market landscape, competitor environment, and dissected ads and communication from existing players to identify the best entry points. King Kong employed the Push-Pull methodology to capture attention and implemented a comprehensive performance marketing solution.
With the groundwork laid, King Kong unleashed their secret weapon – a tantalizing blend of spicy, long-form direct response ads that spread like wildfire and had customers clamoring to get their hands on Scotland Titles’ offerings.
### Results
Scotland Titles achieved $2.2 million USD in revenue within 15 months of launching their new website and King Kong doing the performance marketing, experiencing significant growth and becoming the King of their Jungle.
