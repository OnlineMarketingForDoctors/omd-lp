### Background
Aquatic Performance Training provides aquatic exercise and rehabilitation services. They utilise specialised equipment such as underwater bikes, treadmills, strength training equipment, and nutritional guidance to assist their clients with weight loss/health goals.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
There was a huge need for a better aquatic programs to help people who have injuries, joint replacements, weight limitations that restrict their daily activity and contribute to people feeling helpless, less mobile, decreased self confidence, and increase their chance of health issues. Aquatic Performance Training began with private sessions to small groups and the program continued to grow to over  100 clients a week and renting spaces in other pools. But they needed an aggressive marketing strategy to get the word out there for a unique service without much public awareness and take their business to the next level to open their very own commercial location.
### King Kong’s Approach
Our combined SEO and Facebook covers both people who are actively searching for aquatic based services and those who don’t even know yet that there is an option out there that can help them.
We built an epic funnel to nurture leads and educate them on APT’s unique product and launched Facebook EBook ads with a scroll stopping 2.98% Link Click Through Rate. We generated Free Trial leads from Facebook for a mouth watering low cost of $18.84 and these leads are red hot with 50% of these leads converting into new paying clients.
We have doubled their monthly revenue from $50k to $100k and enabled APT to open up a new centre and increase their capacity 4X!
