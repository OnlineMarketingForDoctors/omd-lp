### Background
Glenvill Homes had a bold vision. Their goal was to simplify the knockdown rebuild process and help Australians transform outdated properties into architecturally designed homes without the usual stress.
But in 2016, they were launching a brand-new website.
No authority.
No traffic.
Only 9 ranking keywords and none on Page 1.
In a competitive construction market, that simply would not generate demand.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Glenvill did not just need more visibility. They needed qualified enquiries from homeowners actively researching knockdown rebuild solutions.
Starting with a fresh domain meant there was no historical authority and no organic baseline to rely on. Meanwhile, established competitors were already dominating suburb-level and service-specific searches.
To compete, Glenvill needed to rank quickly for high-intent, bottom-of-funnel keywords while simultaneously building long-term authority in the space.
### Our Approach
We built their SEO strategy from the ground up.
First, we conducted deep keyword research focused on purchase-ready search terms related to knockdown rebuilds, custom home construction, and suburb-specific queries. These keywords reflected real homeowner intent, not vanity traffic.
We then restructured the website architecture to improve crawlability, internal linking, and user flow. Service pages were re-optimised with clear keyword alignment and stronger calls to action designed to convert visitors into enquiries, not just clicks.
Content played a central role. We developed strategic blog content to educate homeowners in the early research phase, positioning Glenvill as the authority while guiding prospects toward booking consultations.
To strengthen domain authority, we implemented a targeted backlink campaign, securing relevant placements within the building and property sectors to support ranking growth.This was not about incremental improvements. It was about building a scalable organic acquisition engine.
