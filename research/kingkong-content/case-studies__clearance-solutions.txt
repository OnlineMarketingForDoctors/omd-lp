### Background
Clearance Solutions is an online shipping agent business that started in Australia in 2014. They help first time importers with customs and quarantine clearance and shipping solutions.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Clearance Solutions used to get leads from referrals and cold calling. They were able to pay themselves as a single operator but not much more. The challenge was to generate new business and start paid advertising for the first time.
### King Kong’s Approach
We used our direct response style copy to deliver thumb stopping click thru rates of 6.39%.
We built a Micro Website designed to be user centric, focusing on standing out from the crowd and making what can be a complicated process as simple as possible. This Micro Website sucks leads in like a vacuum with a conversion rate of 17.51%.
We have carefully refined our campaign over the years so Clearance Solutions gets highly relevant leads and even has the luxury of turning away work so they can focus on the types of jobs they want.
### Results
In just four short years, we’ve skyrocketed revenue by 339% from $597,685.11 in 2019 to an incredible $2,624,693.37 in 2023! Job profit saw a surge of 212%, soaring from $103,480.68 in 2019 to an unbelievable $323,150.76 by 2023! It’s safe to say, results this good change lives!
