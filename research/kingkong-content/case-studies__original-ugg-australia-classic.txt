### Background
Original UGG Australia Classic had built a steady business with three physical retail stores, focused primarily on their core product: UGG boots. Business was driven almost entirely by foot traffic, with little focus on digital.
But when COVID-19 hit, that model was turned upside down. Overnight, their retail traffic disappeared. It became clear they needed to rapidly shift their focus online, or risk being left behind. That urgency led them to King Kong.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Going digital wasn’t optional, it was survival. The client had concerns. They didn’t want to throw money at ads without knowing what they’d get in return. The market was crowded. The stakes were high. And they didn’t have time to waste on guesswork.
They needed a proven partner to:
- Build a high-performing paid media strategy
- Drive consistent, profitable online sales
- Free up their time to focus on expansion
### Our Approach
We started with a 30-minute discovery call to understand their goals and frustrations. From there, we developed a clear game plan to drive customer acquisition, boost sales, and unlock cash flow.
We focused on ROI-driven PPC campaigns to scale their online presence and maximise returns from every dollar spent. This allowed the client to:
- Step away from the day-to-day ad management
- Invest in new product ranges and stock
- Hire additional staff across fulfilment and retail
- Open new stores with confidence
We brought in our 11 secret herbs and spices to tighten up website checkout and upsell conversion rate, and crafted high-converting ads that cut through a saturated market.
