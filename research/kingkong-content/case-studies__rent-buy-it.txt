### Background
RentBuyit provides their customers with Rent-to-own cars.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
RentBuyIt partnered with us to improve their digital marketing efforts, focusing on enhancing communication and transparency. Our team identified inefficiencies in their keyword, bidding, on-page and off-page existing strategies.
Their primary business goal was to double contracts and sales within 12 months.
### King Kong’s Approach
We implemented our proven strategies to give this campaign a full overhaul. First, we thoroughly analysed their website’s architecture, pinpointing relevant keywords and developing a robust on-page SEO strategy. Our aggressive off-page strategies then boosted their organic search traffic, increasing leads, domain authority, and search relevance.
Next, our Google Ads team conducted in-depth market research to understand RentBuyIt’s ideal customer, identified the exact keywords they are searching to target, and then created compelling direct-response copy that turned cold clicks into hot leads.
### Results
We worked closely together with the RentBuyIt team to install our rapid scaling system in their business and skyrocket their Google rankings, traffic, leads and sales. Together our partnership has generated the following results in less than 1 year.
