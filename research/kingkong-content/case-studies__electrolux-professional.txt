### Background
Before partnering with King Kong, Electrolux Professional relied solely on distributors to promote their products. There was no internal digital marketing strategy, no clarity around lead generation performance, and no ownership over their sales pipeline. The website averaged just five leads per month and functioned more as an online brochure than a business growth tool.
At the time they came to King Kong, the website was generating only five leads a month , with no structure in place to scale. It was acting more like a brochure than a growth engine.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Electrolux Professional wanted to move away from dependency on distributors and turn their own website into a revenue-generating machine. Their primary goal was to rank on Google for relevant keywords with purchase intent, double their leads, and double their sales.
### Our Approach
We launched a targeted SEO strategy designed to drive high-intent organic traffic and convert that traffic into high-quality leads. Our strategy initially focused on 100 carefully selected commercial keywords with strong purchase intent which grew to over 250 throughout the campaign.
We conducted a full 360-degree teardown of the website from on-site technical SEO and content gaps to off-site backlink auditing and authority building.
We executed on-page optimisations such as page title and meta description reviews, keyword density analysis, and fixing internal and external broken links. We created targeted content pages, uploaded monthly SEO content, and conducted both on-site and off-site audits to guide our backlink strategy.
Alongside this, our CRO specialists stepped in to help fix the leaky website, which was limiting conversion and costing Electrolux Professional opportunities.
### Results
With just SEO and a CRO audit, Electrolux Professional transformed their website into a lead generation and sales engine. They saw a 900% lift in leads, generating over €1 million in revenue and over €2.4 million in pipeline opportunities.
Page 1 rankings increased by 1066% and organic traffic not only grew by 53%, but it also became the dominant channel accounting for more than 80% of all site traffic and 90% of leads.Conversion rates increased by 44% and user engagement with Google Maps skyrocketed, with triple digit increases in click throughs, direction requests, and phone calls.
Most notably, Australia became the second top-performing region globally in terms of opportunities generated, outpacing regions far larger and more established.
