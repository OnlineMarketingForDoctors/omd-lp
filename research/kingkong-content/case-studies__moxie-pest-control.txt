### Background
Moxie Pest Control provides pest control services for residential homeowners. They have locations in 15 states across the United States.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Growth In Rankings:
Moxie Pest Control used to generate most of their leads through door to door sales, flyers, mail ads and outbound sales calls. Direct sales started taking up too much of his time, and was becoming expensive.
### Our Approach
We created an SEO strategy for their Oklahoma City location to increase their organic leads that had traditionally been generated through door to door sales, and outbound sales calls. We began with on-page work that helped to improve the site speed, and continued to optimize content that is complementary to our keywords, building a strong organic presence within Oklahoma. From there we took it up a notch with our off page strategy to increase organic conversions, and relevance across Google.
### Results
With our SEO approach, we have achieved insane results. Moxie Pest Control saw a 2,150% increase in page 1 rankings, which resulted in a 108% increase in organic traffic. With the combination of both an increase in rankings and traffic, Moxie Pest Control has been able to generate over $11.8 million in organic revenue alone.

### Conclusion
Organic Search Sessions
In conclusion, our SEO strategy has significantly transformed Moxie Pest Control’s lead generation approach, shifting from traditional methods to a highly effective digital strategy. The remarkable results speak for themselves, with over 2,300 keyword positions gained, a 2,150% increase in page 1 rankings, and a 108% surge in organic traffic. These improvements led to a 90% increase in organic search conversions and generated approximately $11.8 million in organic revenue. This success highlights the power of a well-executed SEO strategy in driving substantial business growth.
