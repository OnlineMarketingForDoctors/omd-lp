### Background
Hurdleys Office Furniture is a business specialising in selling a large range of office furniture DTC and B2B. Despite being known within the market, they still had a relatively small footprint which limited their growth.
Brand Experience
### Challenge
Establish product and brand awareness, gain market share, and achieve profitability early on in a niche market dominated by established competitors who have been active for years.
### Our Approach
In crafting a standout digital marketing strategy for Hurdley’s Office Furniture, King Kong embarked on in-depth analysis of the market dynamics and competitor tactics. By thoroughly evaluating the advertisements and communication strategies of industry leaders, our team pinpointed optimal market entry strategies. We employed the dynamic Push-Pull methodology to engage potential customers effectively, complemented by a robust performance marketing framework designed to maximise reach and conversion. The cornerstone of our approach was the deployment of captivating, long-form direct response ads. These ads, characterised by their compelling content and irresistible appeal, dramatically boosted consumer interest and desire for Hurdley’s innovative office furniture solutions.
### Conclusion
To address the challenges faced by Hurdley’s Office Furniture, King Kong implemented a comprehensive performance marketing solution. We developed and employed SEO, Paid Search and Facebook Ads to drive targeted traffic and revenue to Hurdley’s existing e-commerce site. Long-form and short-form direct response ad copies on Meta were used to generate awareness and interest in office furniture, particularly throughout the Covid-19 pandemic. Through these strategies, King Kong positioned Hurdley’s Office Furniture as a leader in the office furniture market, resulting in significant revenue growth and market share growth.
### Results
King Kong has helped Hurdley’s Office Furniture generate over $2.5 million AUD in revenue over the 5 years King Kong has taken over the performance marketing, experiencing significant growth and becoming the King of their Jungle.
