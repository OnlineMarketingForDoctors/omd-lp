## 5. The Signature Frameworks

### A. The 7-Step Patient Generation System (flagship IP)
1. **Define your "one thing"** — the key procedure that generates most revenue / that you want to be known for, plus target patient profile. (Analogy: McDonald's, Apple, Starbucks are known for one thing, not 20 products.)
2. **Work out the "Why" patients should choose you** — top 3–5 compelling differentiators. "Experienced" and "expert" don't cut through.
3. **Create a killer lead magnet** — an "ethical bribe"; a small commitment ahead of the big one; must target patient pain.
4. **Create a high-converting landing page** for that procedure with the lead magnet — websites have too many competing CTAs; a landing page is "the front view of your shop."
5. **Identify the Dream 100 list** — the top 100 tribes your target audience already belongs to, then infiltrate them. (On some vertical pages this step is "Active Community Outreach.")
6. **Generate raving patient reviews** — 84% of people trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations; OMD offers a plan to generate 20+ reviews in a month.
7. **Refine sales process & optimise conversion rate** — a proven script that does 70% of the convincing before the in-person consult; phone screening, qualifying, objection handling, follow-up.

**The 3 things you need:** (1) affordable, highly targeted traffic; (2) a system to turn traffic into "itchy-to-buy" leads; (3) a sales process and script to convert them.

### B. First-14-days activation plan
1. Activate the existing patient database for immediate bookings
2. Set up a quick referral campaign
3. Website makeover for conversion
4. Online reputation push — more 5-star reviews for promoted services
5. Google Ads, social ads and local SEO campaigns

### C. Signature 9-Step High-Performance SEO Process
SEO Consultation → SEO/Website Audit → Website Design & Conversion Review → Comprehensive Keyword Research → Competitor Analysis → On-Page Optimisation → Off-Page Optimisation / Link Building → Content Marketing → Reporting & Analysis
(Also expressed as: Consultation, Audit, Competitor Analysis, Keyword Research, On-Page, Off-Page, Content, **Local SEO** (GBP/Maps/directories), Performance Monitoring.)

### D. 6-Step Website Design System
Thorough Research → Wireframe Design → Graphic Design → Keyword Research & SEO Setup → Build (desktop + mobile) → Testing & Launching

### E. 5-Step Google Ads Process
1. Check your compliance (Google medical advertising rules)
2. Establish tracking systems
3. Analyse competitors (direct and indirect)
4. Run your first profitable ads
5. Continuously improve and develop campaigns

### F. Case-study campaign methodology (the 8 "Techniques")
Used verbatim across recent case studies — a strong template for LP content:
1. Clarify 3 foundational marketing questions: *Who is their dream patient? Where are they hanging out online? What hooks will get their attention?*
2. Research what strong competitors do and how
3. Website redesign (navigation, CTAs, trust elements, best-sellers, SEO content)
4. Build a sales funnel: **Brand Positioning → Brandscript → One-liner (problem/solution/result) → Landing Pages → Lead Magnet → Follow-up Email Sequence**
5. Dedicated distraction-free landing pages for Google Ads
6. Full-funnel Facebook/Instagram ads (TOF awareness video → MOF retargeting with before/after + testimonials → BOF lead-gen ads with "Free Consultation" CTA)
7. AI automations — SMS bot for instant lead follow-up (<5 minutes)
8. Implement the 9-step High Performance SEO process (GBP reorganisation, local SEO, long-form unique content, keyword research, on-page, link building)

### G. Sales funnel model
Attract Ideal Patients → Pre-Sell Your Services (eBooks, quizzes, lead magnets) → Filter Out Bad-Fit Prospects → Nurture Leads Into Patients

## 15. Objections OMD Pre-empts (useful LP FAQ material)

1. **"When will I see results?"** → Depends on starting point; tangible results typically from month two after full launch.
2. **"How do I know it'll work?"** → Benchmarks set upfront, monthly measurement against KPIs; improvements in Maps rankings, AI Overviews, organic visibility → more enquiries, better leads, more bookings.
3. **"What budget do I need?"** → Minimum A$3,000 (~£1,500/€1,700/US$2,000) per channel per month; marketing is an investment, ROI measured monthly.
4. **"How long is the commitment?"** → 12 months minimum for year one; compounding growth from year two.
5. **"What if it doesn't work?"** → Performance projections upfront; if all recommendations were followed exactly with no improvement, client can request review and early termination.
6. **"We rely on referrals — does online marketing apply to us?"** → Patients research independently before committing; strong online presence wins referral jobs too.
7. **"Why not just run Google Ads?"** → Organic listings still get significantly more clicks (sometimes 30%+); SEO delivers ~2:1 better ROI. You need both.
8. **"Do you guarantee page 1?"** → No honest agency can; OMD points to case studies instead.

## 16. Implications for Landing Page Design

Patterns observed across OMD's own highest-effort pages (Patient Generation Blueprint, AI Assistant, vertical pages) — a house style to follow:

1. **Sticky in-page nav** with anchor links (How It Works · Before & After · 7-Step System · Client Reviews)
2. **Hero:** specific, quantified outcome + named client proof ("How Crown Clinic went from 9 to 28 patients a week in 30 days"), dual CTA, scarcity line
3. **"AS FEATURED IN" logo bar** immediately below hero
4. **Empathy / pain section** written as a letter to the clinic owner — list the frustrations (gaps in schedule, inconsistent bookings, non-serious enquiries, prospects ghosting, colleagues getting busier)
5. **Before & after visual results**
6. **The 7-step system** as tabs or an accordion
7. **Hard numbers**: percentage uplifts, CPL figures, conversion-rate changes with before/after values
8. **Video testimonials** from named surgeons with credentials (MBBS, FRACS, FRCS) and clinic URLs
9. **Founder letter** signed by Huyen Truong, MBA
10. **Cost-of-inaction section** ("What's your potential loss…")
11. **FAQ accordion** covering the eight objections above
12. **Scarcity close:** "We only take on 2 new clients every month"
13. **Compliance badges:** AHPRA · TGA · HIPAA · GDPR · Google Partner
14. **Two-path CTA** where relevant: done-for-you vs DIY resources
15. Multi-currency pricing where pricing is shown (AUD / USD / GBP / EUR) — the audience is global

**Tone reminder:** warm, plain-spoken, slightly irreverent, empathetic to overworked clinicians, relentlessly specific about numbers. Never corporate-bland. Australian/British spelling.
